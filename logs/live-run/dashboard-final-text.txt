## Eight checks on every payment

In the order the contract runs them. A failed check stops the payment and reverts the transaction.

- 1

#### Block exists

The payment names a block the owner created.

- 2

#### Caller is the operator

Only the block's mandate holder can request a payment.

- 3

#### Mandate active and in date

The owner can revoke the mandate at any time. It also stops at its expiry date.

- 4

#### Payee on the allowlist

Money moves only to addresses the owner approved for that block.

- 5

#### Within the per-payment cap

Each payment has a ceiling set by the owner.

- 6

#### Within the lifetime cap

Executed and waiting payments count toward one total limit.

- 7

#### Free cash in the pocket

A block cannot spend cash that is already reserved.

- 8

#### Owner approval above the threshold

Larger payments wait for the owner. Approval checks the mandate and payee again.
